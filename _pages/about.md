Hello! I am an environmental economist who uses tools from trade and industrial organization. I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at the California Institute of Technology. Previously, I was the Globalization Postdoctoral Fellow at Dartmouth College, and I received my PhD in Applied Economics from the Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania, in 2025. You can find my [job market paper](https://mishrap42.github.io/files/PrakashMishra_JMP.pdf) draft here. My dissertation committee consisted of: [Arthur van Benthem](https://www.arthurvanbenthem.com/) (chair), [Gilles Duranton](https://real-faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/duranton/), [Susanna Berkouwer](http://www.susannaberkouwer.com/), and [Ulrich Doraszelski](https://bepp.wharton.upenn.edu/profile/ulrichd/).
