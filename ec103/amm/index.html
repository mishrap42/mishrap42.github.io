Model: households maximise c1−βqβ s.t. c + p(x)q = y − tx; spatial equilibrium V = Ū gives p(x) = p(0)(1 − tx/y)1/β. Developers build floor space h = A Sγ per unit land (γ = 0.7), earn zero profit, and stop where land rent hits rA. Half of the land in the circle is developable. Closed city solves Ū from the population identity by bisection; open city takes Ū as given and solves for L.
